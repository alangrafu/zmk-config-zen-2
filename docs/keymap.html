Proposed keymap — visual review

A redesign built around home-row mods for Shift · Ctrl · Option (Command stays on your left thumb, where you use it most), Miryoku-style thumb layers, and a two-handed mouse layer whose movement reuses your arrow keys — plus scroll, horizontal scroll, back/forward and drag-and-drop. Nothing is written to the keymap yet — this is for you to check placements before I commit. Command, Space, Enter, and left Ctrl are unchanged.

BT Clear is deliberately hard to hit — it's off the key grid entirely and only fires when you press the two outermost Bluetooth keys (BT 0 + BT 4) together while holding the BT layer (Y). No more clearing pairings when reaching for BT 0. Copy / cut / paste / undo / redo stay on the NUM layer over Z X C V B (aligned to the physical ⌘Z/X/C/V keys).

Spanish accents — one press each

On the NUM layer, the left home row becomes the accented vowels in order á é í ó ú, with ñ and ü on the right pinky. Each is a one-press macro that fires the macOS ⌥-deadkey + letter for you — no thinking about which Option to hold.

NUMA S D F G→á é í ó ú
NUM;→ñ · ' → ü

Fallback still works: the raw ⌥-deadkey method (hold the opposite-hand Option — right ⌥/L for é, left ⌥/S for ñ, then the letter). The dedicated keys just save you the reach.

Notes & tuning knobs

  • Home-row mods are Option · Ctrl · Shift on the ring / middle / index (both hands). Command stays on your left thumb, and the pinky keys (A / ;) stay plain — pinky mods misfire the most, so this is more reliable than full Miryoku GACS.
  • Misfire protection: require-prior-idle-ms + positional hold-trigger-key-positions mean the mods only engage with an opposite-hand key, not during fast same-hand rolls. Tunable: tapping-term-ms 180–240.
  • Mouse = hold B (left index), Miryoku-style: right hand does it all — H J K L move (your arrow keys), N M , . scroll, and the clipboard row (Redo · Paste · Copy · Cut · Undo) sits directly above the pointer keys so you can select-with-mouse and copy/paste without leaving the layer. Clicks are on the right thumbs; back / forward / middle-click on the free left fingers. Drag = hold right-thumb L-clk and move. The old right-outer thumb becomes a handy Backspace (easy to change).
  • Media = hold Z, Bluetooth = hold Y — same layer keys you use now. Media is on the right hand with volume rolling on the right pinky (Vol+ top, Vol− home, Mute bottom). BT selection is on the left hand; Studio, &bootloader and &sys_reset live on the BT layer, so flashing no longer needs the physical reset button.
  • Scroll speed (ZMK_POINTING_DEFAULT_SCRL_VAL) may feel fast with smooth-scrolling on; easy to dial down if it overshoots.
